## 1. Nature, convention, and additivity

**Purpose.** This is a single, separate, additive **post-publication closure note**. It durably records the verified publication coordinates of the already-issued Program Authority product-layer acceptance decision `IIPS-V3-NP12-GOVERNED-SCREENER-PRODUCT-ACCEPTANCE-DECISION-01` — a completed governance act — together with the closure facts that follow from its publication.

**What this note is not.** It is **not** an acceptance decision, an attestation, a determination, a certification, or an authorization. It **records publication coordinates only** and expressly does not:

- create, restate, widen, narrow, or reinterpret the acceptance decision, which remains solely the Program Authority's act as recorded in the closed record (§1 of that record holds its verbatim wording, and it is **not** reproduced here);
- confer, extend, or transfer any Program Authority, certification, acceptance, implementation, promotion, deployment, or release authority;
- claim independent technical certification, production readiness, production qualification, or fitness for any purpose;
- determine NP-12 eligibility, admission, or membership under any framework, including NP-13 (§4.9).

**Convention and placement.** The repository's established post-publication closure convention is an additive, documentation-only closure record appended under separate authorization: `docs/integration/IIPS_v3.0_NP12_GOVERNED_SCREENER_PRODUCT_ACCEPTANCE_AND_QUALIFICATION_RECORD.md` §16 (PR #72 — the direct structural precedent for this record's format: provenance table → closure disposition → preserved boundaries → closure summary); `docs/integration/IIPS_v3.0_OWNER_CONTEXT_F3_IMPLEMENTATION_AND_QUALIFICATION_RECORD.md` §9 (PR #69); `docs/integration/IIPS_v3.0_OWNER_CONTEXT_F1_F2_F3_PA_DECISION.md` (PR #64); `docs/integration/IIPS_v3.0_OWNER_CONTEXT_F1_F2_VALUE_ACT.md` (PR #67, post-publication closure section). The closed record's own §11 provides that its publication coordinates *"cannot be recorded inside the artifact that they contain"* and are therefore *"left to a post-publication closure note under the repository's established additive closure convention"*.

**Why a separate file rather than an appended section.** The prior precedents add a closure section to the record being closed. That mechanism is **not available here**: it would require editing the closed record, and the closure direction for this act expressly preserves both canonical records unmodified. The convention's substance — an additive, separately authorized, documentation-only closure record that leaves the closed artifact byte-for-byte intact — is therefore implemented as **one separate additive file**, which is the strictest available form of the same convention. Placed alongside its siblings in `docs/integration/`, the established location for NP-12 and NP-13 governance records.

**Non-interference.** The two canonical records are **not** edited, rewritten, re-rendered, renumbered, moved, or deleted by this note. Their published identities are re-verified unchanged at §2. The status supersession described at §3 is effected **by declaration in this note**, exactly as §16.2(1) of the accepted qualification record did for its own preparation-time statements — never by in-place edit.
