## 6. THE POSITIVE TEST T — COMPLETE NORMATIVE DEFINITION

6.1 **The rule (M-3 semantics).** Unmerged or divergent work **MAY** be a candidate / eligible for consideration **ONLY when the explicitly stated positive test T is satisfied**. T is the **sole** gate: nothing other than T establishes candidacy or consideration eligibility for such work; and — correspondingly — nothing other than T defeats candidacy or consideration eligibility for such work (§10.3).

6.2 **T — definition (governed positive test).** An item of unmerged or divergent work is a **candidate**, and is **eligible for consideration**, under this act **if and only if ALL FOUR conditions below are satisfied and recorded** (evidence per §12):

**T-1 — GOVERNANCE-ACT CONDITION.** An **explicit governance act** exists that, **within its own stated scope, establishes the item's entry into candidate / consideration status**. The act must cite the governance basis under which it is rendered; **its cited basis must itself confer the determination the act performs** — an act whose cited basis does not confer it **cannot satisfy T-1** (the recorded excess-of-cited-basis failure mode, GAP-8, is disqualifying for any act relied upon under T). Substantive determinations remain with the Program Authority under the authority model (`AU-1…AU-4`); "no authority may be inferred" (including from readiness, publication, or circumstance). Status **inferred** from any circumstance satisfies nothing.

**T-2 — IDENTIFICATION CONDITION.** The T-1 act **identifies the item by its exact governed identity**: the item's name/designation **and** its coordinates of record — for lineages: the recorded commit/tree coordinates; for artifacts: blob + byte count + SHA-256; for records: path + ref + status. The identification must be sufficient to distinguish the item from every other item. Identification by class alone, or by circumstance, satisfies nothing.

**T-3 — DOMAIN-STATEMENT CONDITION.** The T-1 act **states, as its own statement (expressly not as an inference)**, that the item is within this act's domain — **unmerged and/or divergent work** — and **records the applicable facts of record** with their evidence coordinates (e.g., non-ancestry of a lineage from the bound operational reference; the recorded divergence facts, including both preserved lineages where applicable), **preserving all such facts without promotion, replacement, or erasure**. Those facts serve **to locate the item in the domain**; they are **not themselves the test** and satisfy no condition beyond this one.

**T-4 — FORM / EFFECTIVE-POINT CONDITION.** The T-1 act carries the framework's **minimum required statements** (`R-4`): the object; the evidence relied on (EC/EV discipline per §12; ref + status for non-`main` material; gaps disclosed); **its effective-point classification under IE-4 / D1-C #4 (explicit; no ambiguity)**; its identity/epoch treatment under the non-inference rule; its express non-decisions; and its durability status. Satisfaction of T-4 does not itself create durability: **no operative effect arises before durable publication + independent remote verification** (`R-5`).

6.3 **Conjunction.** T is satisfied **only** by the conjunction **T-1 ∧ T-2 ∧ T-3 ∧ T-4**, **determined and recorded by explicit governance act**. Candidacy/consideration status is **never** inferred and is **never** conferred by circumstance.

6.4 **Fail-closed default.** Absent a recorded determination satisfying T-1…T-4 for the item: the item is **not** a candidate and **not** eligible for consideration under this act — status `NOT ESTABLISHED` (no negative inference is drawn about the item; the states simply remain unestablished).

6.5 **T is NOT satisfied by — and may not be defined by inference from:** `A1` or any realization fact · repository, ref, or branch presence · Git topology (beyond the T-3 domain statement, which locates but does not qualify) · evidence presence · durability · acceptance-style records · qualification · divergence alone · implementation existence · runtime realization · or any other circumstance. Each of these is **disqualifying as a substitute** for T-1…T-4.

6.6 **Derivation basis of T (corpus anchors — no condition beyond these).** T-1 ← the act requirement and act-establishment discipline (`D0 §2.3`; D3-2 M-1 "Establishment": statuses established **only by an explicit governance act**; `AU-3`; `R-3`/`R-4` pattern; the GAP-8 recorded defect). T-2 ← the identity discipline of record (`D1-C`; `EV-2`: blob + byte count + SHA-256; recorded coordinates as used throughout D3-4/D3-5). T-3 ← the D3-6 domain framework (`NP-13-D3-DEFINITION-01 §10`) and the D3-5-preserved divergence facts (`EC-4`). T-4 ← the framework's minimum act statements (`R-4`), effective-point discipline (`IE-4` / `D1-C #4`), evidence model (§13), and the `R-5` operativity rule. **No external requirement is introduced.**

6.7 **No class-wide candidacy.** Candidacy under T is **item-level only**. This act establishes **no blanket candidacy** for "unmerged work" or "divergent work" as classes, and **no candidacy for any workstream** (NP-09…NP-13) or any named item.
