## 2. Evidence and interpretation notes

### 2.1 Q1–Q2: local use and statutory text

The NSE Terms of Use, clauses 8 and 9, and NSE Copyright policy are linked below. Clause 8 includes storage “in an electronic retrieval system” and hyperlinks in its restrictions; the Copyright policy separately describes personal, non-commercial downloads, while excluding third-party-copyright content from that permission. Those texts do not answer whether the intended local, single-user app falls within the clause 8 term or how the separate download language applies to issuer-authored reports.

Section 52(1)(a)(i) of the Copyright Act, 1957 refers to fair dealing for “private or personal use, including research”; its Explanation says electronic storage for a purpose in that clause is not infringement. The addendum records those words without deciding their application to a given work or whether/how website terms may operate alongside the statute. No automatic exemption or contract override is asserted.

### 2.2 Q3–Q4: source choice is not a rights finding

The reviewed BSE disclaimer states that proprietary rights in website information remain with BSE and restricts reproduction, redistribution or transmission; the linked copyright notice also restricts reproduction without written consent. The reviewed pages did not establish a personal-download permission. For any BSE-only security, the issuer’s IR source is the chosen operational source, with BSE used to locate or cross-check the filing. That choice does not resolve rights in the document or terms governing its access.

The AGI IR page was reviewed as an example relevant to the pre-existing Principal-reported AGI source package only. It provides a financial-year-organized investor-document listing, including financial results and report categories. The reviewed Privacy Policy principally describes personal-data processing and website interactions; the reviewed Disclaimer addresses website content/images and gives no clear financial-document download licence. This limited review is not a finding that no separate, document-specific or other AGI terms exist. It cannot be generalized to another issuer. AGI is not part of O3 and is not admitted by this addendum; local AGI documents or analysis are not repository evidence unless independently supplied and verified.

### 2.3 Q5: evidence of requests, not an adjudication

The existing records under `evidence/real-issuer/` are retained as historical evidence. In particular, the WI12-L039 run records include UTC run stamps of `2026-11-08T05:19:26Z` and `2026-11-08T05:23:41Z` and record automated retrieval/processing of NSE-hosted XBRL/iXBRL endpoints; the WI12-L038 record also has an 8 November 2026 UTC run stamp. These logs evidence the recorded activity; the timestamps are the logged run times, not a legal conclusion about when a contractual violation occurred. The prior source-rights assessment also records NSE endpoint activity and explicitly leaves breach undecided.

For future handling: no repeat automated NSE retrieval. Clause 8 expressly names hyperlinks, so do not add or publish direct NSE filing links. This does not erase existing historical endpoint/link evidence, determine whether any past act breached a term, or resolve the legal interpretation.
