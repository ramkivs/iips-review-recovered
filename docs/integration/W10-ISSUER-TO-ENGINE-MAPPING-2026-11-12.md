# W10 — issuer-to-engine field mapping and qualification (2026-11-12)

**Status: COMPLETE.** This document is the auditable, field-level mapping of every input declared by the thirteen certified sector engines, together with the rules the integrated issuer-to-engine qualification applies to each one. The matrix in §7 is generated from the executing registry (`iips-platform/src/investment/qualification/mapping.ts`) and is asserted byte-for-byte by the W10 suite, so the published mapping and the mapping the code applies are the same artefact.

Qualification is a **readiness** statement: it says whether each engine’s declared inputs can be resolved from governed IID evidence at an evaluated period. It publishes no score, verdict, rank, target price or recommendation, and it does not execute any engine. No golden, expected-output or validation fixture is used as issuer evidence anywhere in this work.

## 1. Scope and authority

- **Engines covered:** exactly the thirteen entries of the frozen `CERTIFIED_ENGINES` registry (`iips-platform/src/integration/EngineRegistry.ts`). The W10 suite asserts that the qualification registry and the certified registry name the same engines, with the same IES, sector family, engine version and calibration profile.
- **Inputs covered:** every property declared by each engine’s `…Input` interface, including declared decision-override flags. Where an interface also declares an index signature, that signature is recorded as `hasIndexSignature` and is **not** treated as permission to pass unlisted keys; only declared fields are mapped, qualified or forwarded.
- **Not in scope:** any change to a sector engine, its calibration, its scoring or decision path; any W11 work; Dhan live integration; the IPD; and any reinterpretation of W05 or W07 evidence. Nothing under `iips-platform/src/sector-engines` or `iips-platform/src/integration` is modified, and the A13 protected-surface guard is untouched.

## 2. How the mapping was built

Each declared input was traced through the frozen engine in three steps, and only findings that survived all three were recorded:

1. **Declaration.** The field was read from the engine’s `…Input` interface, with its declared type and optionality.
2. **Consumption.** The field was traced into the engine’s metrics, scoring, calibration and decision code to establish whether the path actually reads it, how many times, with what transform, and against which calibration band table. This step is why the registry distinguishes `DECLARED_UNUSED` (declared, never read) and `CONFLICT` (read, but the canonical quantity the engine expects cannot be reproduced without an undeclared transform) from `MAPPED`/`DERIVED`.
3. **Evidence.** A source was authorized only where a governed IID contract supplies exactly that quantity: a canonical line item through the closed line-item table, a canonical operating metric through the closed `MetricId` table (with its unit, range and minimum source class), or a statement-derived ratio whose inputs are canonical line items and whose formula is declared on the field. No mapping was inferred from a similar-looking name, and no mapping was authorized by a calibration band that merely happens to fit.

Derived ratios are computed by the qualification itself (seven declared derivations) and published with `basis=DERIVED`, their formula, their inputs and their declared deviation. Nothing is backfilled into the dataset, and a derived value is never presented as a filed figure.

This is the mechanism by which the inventory’s risk register became machine-enforced rather than prose.

## 7. The matrix

One identity table and one semantics table per engine. ## 8. Fail-closed rules applied by the qualification

Every field resolves to exactly one outcome, published with a code set, a note and (where a value exists) the evidence that produced it: the period, statement scope, basis, presence, reason, source reference, filing reference and the observations a derivation consumed.

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `RESOLVED` | A governed value exists at the evaluated period, scope and basis, and passes every check. |
| `MISSING` | The source exists in the contracts but carries no record for this issuer; absence is not zero. |
| `NIL_NOT_ZERO` | The source positively declares nil; a nil is not a number. |
| `STALE` | The latest evidenced period is outside the frozen freshness window (P&L 200 days, balance sheet 250 days). |
| `SCOPE_MISMATCH` / `PERIOD_MISMATCH` | The value exists only at another statement scope or another period, and is not substituted. |
| `INVALID` | The value exists but fails a contract check (unit, range, validation state, source class, imputation, future observation, ambiguity, cross-field contradiction). |
| `DERIVATION_BLOCKED` | A declared derivation cannot be computed (missing input, zero denominator, negative base). |
| `DERIVATION_NOT_PERMITTED` | The derivation is a closing-balance ratio and the policy does not permit that declared deviation. |

## 9. W07 and W05 interplay

The qualification consumes the W07 capital-structure block unchanged (`capital-structure/1.0.0`) rather than recomputing debt:

- Gross debt is the W07 aggregate over short-term borrowings, current maturities, long-term borrowings and lease liabilities. A component that is absent blocks the aggregate; a component the filer declared nil is counted as zero only under the W07 aggregation contract, and the nil keeps its source and period.
- Net debt is never substituted for gross debt. Where W07 cannot build net debt (for example the unresolved KEC `CURRENT_INVESTMENTS`), the qualification simply does not need it: no engine input is mapped to net debt.
- A leverage multiple is refused when the debt instant differs from the period the EBITDA belongs to. W07 will resolve borrowings from the latest year that reported them; the qualification will not divide this year’s earnings by last year’s balance sheet, so a missing current-year borrowing block yields `PERIOD_MISMATCH`, not a stale ratio.
- The unresolved Marico `PREFERENCE_CAPITAL` (W07 `L-038`) and KEC `CURRENT_INVESTMENTS` (W07 `L-039`) remain unresolved and unvalued here. Neither is mapped to any engine input, and neither is turned into a zero by this work.

## 10. What this document and the qualification do not do

- They do not score, rank, value or recommend any issuer, and they do not assert that a qualified engine would produce a particular verdict.
- They do not claim that a `MAPPED` field is economically identical to the label the frozen engine uses: the mapping states the canonical quantity, its unit and the transform, and it preserves every known semantic gap as `CONFLICT` or as an explicit note.
- They do not authorize any currently unmapped or conflicting input. Mapping a conflict remains a methodology decision for its owner, not a qualification concern.
- They do not validate the frozen calibration. Where a calibration band table is inconsistent (for example a band boundary that cannot be reached, or a field reused from the wrong table), the registry records the fact on the field and the qualification refuses only where the *input* cannot be evidenced. Scoring defects inside a frozen engine are outside W10’s authority to fix and are not silently worked around.
