## 2. Sector economics

How Technology businesses create and earn value:
- **Primary revenue model:** varies radically by subsegment — subscription (SaaS), license, usage-based, transaction/platform fees, hardware/product sales, foundry/manufacturing, services/project fees, managed services.
- **Cost structure:** R&D intensity, software gross margins (high), hardware COGS, distribution/sales, customer acquisition.
- **Value chain:** R&D/IP → product/platform development → distribution/cloud → customer adoption → renewal/retention (recurring) or one-time sale.

## 5. Economic drivers

- R&D intensity, product innovation, recurring revenue base, retention/renewal, switching costs, network effects, platform lock-in, customer concentration, capex (semis/hardware), cloud/usage growth.

## 6. Value drivers

- **Recurring / subscription revenue** — durable, high-margin.
- **Retention / renewal rates** — net revenue retention (NRR).
- **Switching costs / platform lock-in** — customer stickiness.
- **IP / R&D pipeline** — innovation → growth.
- **Network effects** — value grows with users/participants.
- **Customer concentration** — diversification of revenue base.
- **Gross margin** — software vs hardware economics.
- **What destroys value:** churn/retention decline, customer-concentration loss, disruptive obsolescence, over-spend (R&D/capex), pricing pressure, cyclical hardware/semi downturn.

## 7. Risk / regulatory structure

- IP/patent, export controls (semis), data privacy/security (GDPR/CCPA), antitrust (platforms), cybersecurity regulation, cloud/infra compliance, customer concentration risk.

## 8. Lifecycle characteristics

- **Growth-heavy** (software/platform/cloud) vs **cyclical** (semis/hardware) vs **mature** (IT services).
- Rapid innovation cycles; disruption risk; network-effect compounding.
- Not a single homogeneous "Technology" lifecycle — varies by subsegment.

## 10. UIO / CSIP mapping direction

Technology maps to the Universal Investment Ontology (Conviction, Confidence, Quality, Growth, Risk, Valuation, Capital Efficiency, Moat) via ontology registration — so CSIP can consume it with **zero CSIP change**.

- Quality: recurring revenue, retention, IP, switching costs.
- Growth: recurring revenue growth, usage, network effects.
- Risk: customer concentration, cyclicality (semis/hardware), disruption.
- Valuation: EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, FCF multiple, P/S (growth).
- Capital Efficiency: FCF yield, cash conversion.
- Moat: network effects, switching costs, IP, platform lock-in.
