# WI-5 — commands actually run

Working directory `frontend/` unless stated; `iips-platform/` for the ingestion CLI. Nothing below touches the
network: the two statement text layers were fetched once, outside the product, and the extracts under `source/`
were committed before any of these commands ran. The one market observation is a recorded quote, not a live call.

## 1 — the declarations in the input

`ingest-input/periods.csv` holds three consecutive audited consolidated periods of eClerx Services Limited
(FY2024, FY2023, FY2022) in Rs million (`source_scale = MILLION`, `taxonomy = INDAS`, `basis = AS_FILED`).

Exactly one cell declares a nil: the FY2024 row's `PREFERENCE_CAPITAL` carries

    NIL: The Company has only one class of equity shares having a par value of Rs 10 per share.

Every other cell is either a filed number or empty. The borrowings columns are numbers in all three periods:
FY2024 carries the filed dash as an explicit zero (`0`), FY2023 6.77 / 3.08, FY2022 0.50 / 0.91 — no nil is
declared for borrowings because the consolidated notes contain no sentence declaring borrowings nil
(`source/consol-fy24-note-40-financial-risk-management.txt` records the exclusion-style wording that exists
instead). `CURRENT_MATURITIES_OF_LONG_TERM_DEBT` is empty in all three periods: the filer presents no such caption.
