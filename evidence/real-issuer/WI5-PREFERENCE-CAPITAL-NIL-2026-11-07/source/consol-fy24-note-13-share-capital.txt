223 ECLERX SERVICES LIMITED | Consolidated Financial Statements

Terms / rights attached to equity shares

The Company has only one class of equity shares having a par value of Rs 10 per share. Each holder of
equity shares is entitled to one vote per equity share. The Company declares and pays dividends in
Indian rupees. The dividend proposed by the Board of Directors is subject to the approval of the
shareholders in the ensuing Annual General Meeting.

--- what this establishes ---

The authorised share capital of the Company consists of a single class: equity shares of Rs. 10 each.
No preference share capital is authorised, issued, subscribed or paid up in either period presented,
and the note states in the filer's own words that the Company has only one class of equity shares.

--- what this does NOT establish ---

It says nothing about borrowings, debt, or any other governed line item. It does not state that
preference capital is nil in the word "nil"; it states the class position, which is the same form of
wording that WI-3 accepted for Infosys ("The Company has only one class of shares referred to as
equity shares having a par value of Rs 5/-.").
