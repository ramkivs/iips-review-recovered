throwaway workflow, exactly as WI-8 did:

      pdftotext -layout -f <page> -l <page> <report>.pdf -

directory on the branch (artifact downloads are not reachable from the sandbox). Run `42375363922`. The hit report is
`source/caption-search-log.txt`; the scratch directory and the workflow were deleted before publication.
