# Reading of the result, stated here so the log is not over-read:
#   * `current_maturities` occurs on exactly five pages. Three carry an amount disclosure inside Note 13(a):
#     FY2023-24 p.191 and FY2022-23 p.149 carry the "scheduled maturity of long term borrowings" table (its first

#     (FY2023-24 p.209 and FY2022-23 p.170) carry the phrase only as checklist item "(v) Current maturities of long
#     term borrowings" inside the Schedule III additional-information sheet; no group amount is printed there and no
#     figure is taken from them.
#   * `net_debt` occurs on two pages, both in the FY2022-23 report: p.163 (Note 29 Capital Management, consolidated) and
#     p.209 (the same note in the company statements). The note defines the term — "Net financial debt is defined as
#     current and non current borrowings" — so what the filing calls net debt is BORROWING-ONLY: it subtracts no cash,
#     includes no leases and is not the governed net debt. On p.163 only the ratio row (0.12 / 0.10) survives the layout
#     extraction; the amounts (59 / 95, at company level, with total equity 3,677 / 3,049 and ratio 0.02 / 0.03) are on
#     p.209. The FY2023-24 report carries the string on no page at all.
#   * every `net_cash` hit is auditor-report wording ("net cash outflows"), the acquisition note ("Net cash paid") or the
#     KPI table ("Net Cash Flow from Operations per share"), not a management net-debt/net-cash position.
#   * `preference\s+share` occurs on four pages, and every hit is the same table: Note 12(a)'s AUTHORISED share
#     capital, which lists "Preference shares of Rs 10/- each 7 / 65" beside the two equity classes. The note's issued,
#     subscribed and paid-up table lists equity shares only (1,29,41,01,828 shares, total 129 / 129) and prints no
#     preference row — not even a dash. So no issued preference capital exists to map, and nothing states in words that
#     it is nil; reading zero out of the fact that the issued total equals the equity line would be an inference from a
#     total, which D-2 forbids. PREFERENCE_CAPITAL therefore stays NOT_REPORTED.
#   * `debenture` occurs only in statutory/auditor language; no debenture line exists.
#   * every `Total Borrowings` hit is a column head of the lease-liability reconciliation, not a total-debt figure.
