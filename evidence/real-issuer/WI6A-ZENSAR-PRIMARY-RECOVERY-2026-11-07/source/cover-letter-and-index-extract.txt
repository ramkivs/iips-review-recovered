A.3 WHY THIS MATTERS — the scope arithmetic
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The filing publishes TWO separate sets of audited financial statements, one after the other:

    Standalone Financial Statements   printed pages 176 .. 247
    Consolidated Financial Statements printed pages 248 .. 321   (Glossary begins at 322)

The owner reports the Zensar sentence at printed pages ~246-247:

    "The Company does not have any outstanding borrowings as at 31 March 2024 and 31 March 2023."

Printed pages 246-247 sit BELOW the consolidation boundary at page 248. On the document's own architecture the
sentence is therefore part of the STANDALONE notes, i.e. it describes Zensar Technologies Limited alone and says
nothing about its subsidiaries or about the Group. Under D-2 a standalone declaration cannot be transferred to
consolidated data, so the sentence can never carry a consolidated nil regardless of what the consolidated notes
say.
