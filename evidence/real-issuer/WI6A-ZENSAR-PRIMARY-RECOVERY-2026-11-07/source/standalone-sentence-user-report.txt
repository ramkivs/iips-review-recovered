THE SENTENCE (owner-reported, verbatim)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "The Company does not have any outstanding borrowings as at 31 March 2024 and 31 March 2023."

  Reported locator: Notes to the Standalone Financial Statements, printed pages ~246-247.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCOPE DETERMINATION — settled on the document's own pagination
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Standalone Financial Statements begin at printed page 176
  Consolidated Financial Statements begin at printed page 248     (source/cover-letter-and-index-extract.txt, A.2)

  The sentence sits at printed pages 246-247, i.e. BEFORE the consolidation boundary at page 248.

  => statement_scope = STANDALONE
  => subject of the sentence = Zensar Technologies Limited, the legal entity
  => the sentence says nothing about subsidiaries, joint ventures, or the Group

A standalone declaration must not be transferred to consolidated data. This is the central constraint of
WI-6A and it holds whatever the consolidated notes turn out to contain. Even if an identical sentence were
later found inside the consolidated section, the standalone occurrence would still not be the evidence for it:
the consolidated occurrence would have to be read, quoted and page-anchored in its own right.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECOND REASON IT CANNOT CARRY A NIL IN THIS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The text above is the owner's transcription. Under D-2 a nil must be carried by wording that the governed run
read from the audited note and anchored by printed page and note number. A transcription supplied in
conversation is a locator, not evidence: it tells the next pass where to look, and nothing more.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE ANSWER (and what would not)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WOULD: reading printed pp.248-321 of the FY2023-24 filing and finding, in the consolidated notes, an affirmative
sentence about consolidated borrowings for both 31 March 2024 and 31 March 2023, quoted verbatim with page and
note number, plus two preceding audited consolidated periods on the same basis.

WOULD NOT: this standalone sentence; a dash or an absent caption in the consolidated balance sheet; equity
reconciling arithmetically; an auditor's remark; a Management Discussion and Analysis statement; a secondary
aggregator's rendering of the notes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A CAUTION AGAINST THE TRANSFER, RECORDED AS A CAUTION ONLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Zensar's own consolidation working papers show that the group is not obviously debt-free at subsidiary level:
the special-purpose financial statements of Zensar Technologies Inc. prepared "for the purpose of consolidation
by its Holding Company (Zensar Technologies Limited)" show a current-borrowings line reading 110 / 140 (amounts
and units as rendered by the aggregator, not verified) alongside lease liabilities. That is a reproduction of a filing by a market-data
aggregator, undated against the audited set, and it is therefore NOT evidence of anything. It is recorded for
one purpose only: to show that importing a parent-only "no borrowings" sentence into group figures is not a
harmless assumption. It does not establish a consolidated borrowing balance, and no number from it appears in
any dataset.
