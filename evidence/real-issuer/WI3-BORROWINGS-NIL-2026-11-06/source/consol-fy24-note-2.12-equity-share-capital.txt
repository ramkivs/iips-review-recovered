The Company's objective when managing capital is to safeguard its ability to continue as a going concern and to
maintain an optimal capital structure so as to maximize shareholder value. In order to maintain or achieve an optimal

shares or buy back issued shares. As of March 31, 2024, the Company has only one class of equity shares and has no

READING NOTE. The nil used for `PREFERENCE_CAPITAL` is the first quoted sentence:

    The Company has only one class of shares referred to as equity shares having a par value of ₹5/-.

SCOPE NUANCE, recorded so it is not glossed: that sentence is about "the Company" (the parent), while the capital
block it feeds is the consolidated one. It is applied to the consolidated block because the consolidated statement of
equity and liabilities shows exactly two equity lines for the owners — equity share capital (₹2,071 crore) and other
equity (₹86,045 crore) — plus non-controlling interests (₹345 crore), and no preference instrument appears anywhere in
the consolidated balance sheet. The capital-management paragraph immediately above adds, for the same date, "As of
March 31, 2024, the Company has only one class of equity shares and has no debt."
