## 1 — the declarations in the input

`ingest-input/periods.csv` is the Work Item 1 file with four columns appended (`LONG_TERM_BORROWINGS`,
`SHORT_TERM_BORROWINGS`, `CURRENT_MATURITIES_OF_LONG_TERM_DEBT`, `PREFERENCE_CAPITAL`). The 2024-03-31 CONSOLIDATED
row carries, in each of the four cells, `NIL: <the filer's sentence>`; the FY23 and FY22 rows carry nothing, so the
older periods stay `NOT_REPORTED`. The ingester refuses a bare `NIL` (`NIL_WITHOUT_QUOTED_WORDING`) and a nil with no
`filing_ref` (`NIL_WITHOUT_PROVENANCE`); both guards are exercised by the WI-3 test.
