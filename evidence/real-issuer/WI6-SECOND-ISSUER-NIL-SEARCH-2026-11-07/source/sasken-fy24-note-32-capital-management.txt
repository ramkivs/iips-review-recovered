32.  Capital management
The Group's policy is to maintain a strong capital base so as to maintain investor, creditor and market confidence and
to sustain future development of the business. Management monitors the return on capital as well as the level of
dividends to equity shareholders.
The Group monitors capital using a ratio of 'adjusted net debt' to 'adjusted equity'. For this purpose, adjusted net debt
is defined as total liabilities less cash and cash equivalents. Adjusted equity comprises all components of equity, other
than amounts accumulated in the hedging reserve.
The Group's policy is to keep the ratio below 2.00.
