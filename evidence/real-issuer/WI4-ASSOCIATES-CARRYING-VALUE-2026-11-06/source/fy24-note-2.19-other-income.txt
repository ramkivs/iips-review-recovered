Accounting policy — Other income is comprised primarily of interest income, dividend income, gain/loss on investment and
exchange gain/loss on forward and options contracts and on translation of foreign currency assets and liabilities.
Interest income is recognized using the effective interest method. Dividend income is recognized when the right to
receive payment is established.

OBSERVATION RECORDED WITH THE EXTRACT (not part of the filed text)
This is the note that would carry a "share of profit/(loss) of associates and joint ventures" credit if any
equity-accounted interest existed, because Ind AS 28 requires that share to be presented as a separate line item of
profit or loss (and the note disaggregates the whole of other income). The complete note contains seven components —
interest at amortised cost, interest at FVOCI, gains on investments at FVTPL, exchange gains on forwards/options,
exchange gains on translation, interest on income-tax refund, and miscellaneous income — and none of them is a share of
an associate's or joint venture's result. The two rows whose labels are printed out of order in the filed text layer
(the ₹100 / ₹(647) row and the blank-labelled exchange rows) belong to those same seven components; the filed total of
4,711 / 2,701 ties to the statement of profit and loss.
