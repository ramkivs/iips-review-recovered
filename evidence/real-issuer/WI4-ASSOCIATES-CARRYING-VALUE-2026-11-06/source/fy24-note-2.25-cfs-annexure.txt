OBSERVATION RECORDED WITH THE EXTRACT (not part of the filed text)
This annexure is the one table in the consolidated statements that is required to itemise the composition of the Group's
consolidated net assets, profit or loss, OCI and total comprehensive income. Its blocks are exactly: Infosys Limited,
Indian Subsidiaries, Foreign Subsidiaries — then Subtotal, consolidation adjustment, Controlled Trusts and
Non-controlling interests. There is no associates block and no joint-ventures block, and the Subtotal of 99,492 is
described as 100% of consolidated net assets before the adjustment — an associates/JV block at nil would still have to
be listed if the Group held one. This is the strongest single confirmation that no equity-accounted interest is
recognised at 2024-03-31.
