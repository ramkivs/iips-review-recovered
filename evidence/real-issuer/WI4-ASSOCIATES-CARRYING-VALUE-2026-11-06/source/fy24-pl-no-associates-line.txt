OBSERVATION RECORDED WITH THE EXTRACT (not part of the filed text)
The statement of profit and loss has no "Share of profit/(loss) from associates and joint ventures" line, and the
other-comprehensive-income section has no "share of other comprehensive income of associates and joint ventures"
line. Ind AS 1 (para 82(c)) and Ind AS 28 require both to be presented separately whenever an equity-accounted
interest exists. Their absence is consistent with the balance sheet: no equity-accounted interest is recognised in
any of the two years presented.
