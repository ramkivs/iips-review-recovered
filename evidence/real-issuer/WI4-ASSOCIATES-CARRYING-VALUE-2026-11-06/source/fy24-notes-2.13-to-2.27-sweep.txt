The only occurrence of the word "associated" anywhere in these notes that carries any weight is a tax phrase, quoted
verbatim from the Note 2.24.1 footnote (page 48):

  "These claims are on account of issues of disallowance of expenditure towards software being held as capital in
   nature, payments made to associated enterprises held as liable for withholding of taxes, among others."

"Associated enterprises" there is Indian income-tax terminology (transfer pricing / withholding on payments to
non-residents). It names no entity, asserts no relationship, carries no balance, and has nothing to do with an Ind AS 28
associate.

OBSERVATION RECORDED WITH THE EXTRACT (not part of the filed text)
Nothing in Notes 2.13 to 2.27 discloses an associate or a joint venture, a dormant associate, an equity-method balance,
or a share of an associate's result. The two notes that could have carried such a disclosure — 2.19 (the only
disaggregation of profit or loss beyond revenue and expenses) and 2.25 (the entire related-party universe, including the
group-structure annexure) — are both complete above and contain none. The dormancy markers in 2.25 ("Under liquidation",
"Liquidated effective …") attach to subsidiaries that are already consolidated line by line, which is the opposite of a
dormant associate outside consolidation.

Coverage note: Notes 2.21 (leases) and 2.22 (employee benefits) occupy pages 41–46 with movement tables for right-of-use
assets, lease liabilities, defined benefit obligations and share-based payment plans. Their opening tables and closing
pages were read; their interior pages were not re-read, because neither note can present an equity-accounted investment
— both are reconciliations of balances that the balance sheet and Note 2.11 already account for, and neither is a
vehicle for third-party equity interests. This is recorded as a limit rather than glossed over.
