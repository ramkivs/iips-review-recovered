OBSERVATION RECORDED WITH THE EXTRACT (not part of the filed text)
Every row of this note is a financial instrument measured at amortized cost, at fair value through other
comprehensive income, or at fair value through profit or loss. There is no equity-method row and no
associates/joint-venture row. The note reconciles both ways:
  non-current 11,708 + current 12,915 = 24,623 total investments; and
  amortized cost 1,759 + FVOCI 19,620 + FVTPL 3,244 = 24,623.
The note is therefore a complete decomposition of the two balance-sheet captions: no amount measured under
Ind AS 28 (equity method) is included in them.
