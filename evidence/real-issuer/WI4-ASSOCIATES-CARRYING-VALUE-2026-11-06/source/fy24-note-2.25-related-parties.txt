OBSERVATION RECORDED WITH THE EXTRACT (not part of the filed text)
This is the complete structure of the note, and it has exactly three related-party groups: (i) subsidiaries — a single
list, every row 100%-owned or majority-owned and controlled, with holdings shown for 2024 and 2023; (ii) trusts —
post-employment benefit plans, four controlled trusts and Infosys Foundation (a trust jointly controlled by KMPs);
(iii) key management personnel and their compensation. There is no "associates" list, no "joint ventures" list and no
"entities over which KMP exercise significant influence" list. The parenthetical "(22) Under liquidation" marks
subsidiaries that are under liquidation — a dormancy marker on entities that are already consolidated line by line,
not on an associate. No equity-method investment, no carrying amount, no additions, disposals or impairment of an
associate appears anywhere in the note.
