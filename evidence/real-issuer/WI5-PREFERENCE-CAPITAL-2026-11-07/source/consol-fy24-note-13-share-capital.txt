223 ECLERX SERVICES LIMITED | Consolidated Financial Statements

Terms / rights attached to equity shares

The Company has only one class of equity shares having a par value of Rs 10 per share. Each holder of
equity shares is entitled to one vote per equity share. The Company declares and pays dividends in
Indian rupees. The dividend proposed by the Board of Directors is subject to the approval of the
shareholders in the ensuing Annual General Meeting.

--- what this establishes ---

That the Company's equity shares are a single class of Rs 10 shares carrying one vote per share and rupee dividends.
The table shows authorised capital of 100,000,000 shares of Rs 10 each (Rs 1,000.00 million) and issued capital of
48,232,242 shares (Rs 482.32 million) at March 31, 2024.

--- what this does NOT establish ---

Anything about preference shares. The sentence sits under the heading "Terms / rights attached to equity shares" and
speaks about classes of EQUITY shares; it does not say the Company has one class of shares, nor that it has no
preference shares. Note 13's tables contain no preference-share line.

This matters because the WI-5 bundle as first committed recorded PREFERENCE_CAPITAL as a nil on this sentence. That
declaration was withdrawn on 2026-11-07: presentation and completeness are not a declaration, and this bundle will not
turn one into the other. See source/preference-capital-investigation.txt for the pages read and the reasoning.

The comparison drawn earlier — that this is "the same form of wording WI-3 accepted for Infosys" — was wrong. Infosys's
accepted wording is "the Company has only one class of shares referred to as equity shares", a statement about all
classes of shares; this one is narrower.
