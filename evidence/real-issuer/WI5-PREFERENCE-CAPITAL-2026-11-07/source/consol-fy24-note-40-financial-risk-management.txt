40. Financial risk management objectives and policies

The Group's principal financial liabilities, other than derivatives and lease liabilities comprises
trade and other payables. The main purpose of these financial liabilities is to finance the Group's
operations. The Group's principal financial assets include trade and other receivables, and cash and
cash equivalents and other bank balances that derive directly from its operations. The Group also
holds FVTPL investments and enters into derivative transactions. The Group is exposed to market risk,
credit risk and liquidity risk. [...]

--- what this establishes ---

The note characterises the Group's financial liabilities as derivatives, lease liabilities and trade
and other payables. It does not say the Group "has no borrowings": the exclusions are grammatical, not
a declared nil, and a dash balance in the balance sheet is a filed zero rather than a statement.

--- why this matters to WI-5 ---

This is the reason WI-5 declares no nil on LONG_TERM_BORROWINGS, SHORT_TERM_BORROWINGS or
CURRENT_MATURITIES_OF_LONG_TERM_DEBT for this issuer. Reading a nil out of an exclusion list would be
exactly the inference D-2 refuses (NIL_WITHOUT_QUOTED_WORDING at the grammar level, and an inferred
zero at the evidence level).
