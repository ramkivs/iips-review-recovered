Note 29.1:
During the year ended March 31, 2022, the Group had received `0.50 crore towards government grant from Government of Rajasthan
for setting up an Oxygen plant under Special package for Medical oxygen. The Group has amortised the grant based on useful life
of the plant and recognised income for current year of ` 0.02 crore (previous year ` 0.02 crore) under other income (Refer note No
40). The balance amount of grant is shown as “Deferred Grant” in non-current liability of ` 0.39 crore (previous year ` 0.41 crore)
and other current liability of ` 0.02 crore (Refer Note 36). The Group does not have any unfulfilled conditions or other contingencies
attached to the same.
