13.                                                MANAGEMENT OF CAPITAL
The Company’s objectives when managing capital are to provide the shareholder with a rate of return on its investment commensurate
with the level of risk assumed. However, the parent company passed a resolution on 28 March 2024 to liquidate the Company, and
accordingly the Company is no longer actively managing its capital to achieve this objective.

Capital, which is unchanged from the previous year, comprises equity funds as presented in the statement of financial position. Debt
comprises total amounts owed by the Company, net of cash and cash equivalents.
