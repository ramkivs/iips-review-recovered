The claims provision represents the total provision recognized to cover expected claims from a third party in relation to the
Company’s activities.

Based on the technical consultations obtained, management believes that the value of such claims will not exceed the provision recognized.

The disclosures normally required for provisions in accordance with Egyptian Accounting Standard No. (28) “Provisions, Contingent
Liabilities and Contingent Assets” have not been presented, as the Company believes that such disclosure could seriously prejudice the
outcome of the ongoing negotiations with the relevant parties.

Management reviews this provision on an annual basis and adjusts the recognized amount in light of the latest developments,
discussions, and agreements reached with those parties.

25- Lease liabilities
The company made a lease contract and the right of use assets were recorded at the current value of the lease payments according
to the contract, also the company signed a lease contract for a unit in the Katameya investment area, the Ring Road, to be used as an
administrative headquarters office for a period of 5 years ending in 2025, and the right of use assets have been recorded at the present
value of the lease payments according to the contract.

the company has entered into a lease agreement for a unit located in the Sheraton area – ground floor – plot 1 – square 1300 –
Ankara street – to be used as its administrative headquarters for a period of five years commencing on 1 September 2025 and ending
on 1 September 2030. right-of-use assets have been recognized at the present value of the lease payments in accordance with the
lease agreement.

These lease liabilities are measured at the present value of the remaining lease payments, discounted using the lessee’s incremental
borrowing rate. the weighted average lessee’s incremental borrowing rate is applied to the lease liabilities.
