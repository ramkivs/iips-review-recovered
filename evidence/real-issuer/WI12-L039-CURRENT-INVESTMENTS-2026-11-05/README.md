# L-039: subsequent exchange disclosures and the machine-readable zero

## Determination and boundary

**No mapping is authorized on the evidence verified here.** The existing 31 March 2026 KEC consolidated dataset remains `CURRENT_INVESTMENTS = MISSING / NOT_REPORTED`; `L-039` remains **OPEN**. This is a negative mapping determination, not a finding that the exchange filing contains no zero: the audited consolidated NSE XBRL instance contains an actual `in-capmkt:CurrentInvestments = 0`, and the corresponding rendered document displays `Current investments 0.00`. This is material new evidence and must not be suppressed or described as merely a missing caption. A numeric reported zero is distinct from both absence and `NIL_BY_DISCLOSURE` in the existing contract, and, if an authoritative and properly contextualized reported zero is established, can be `PRESENT` and close the bridge without a nil-aggregation policy change.

The runner reports establish the instance's filing row and value, but **do not extract and verify the fact's context, dimensions, unit, precision or source-generation semantics**. The template also renders numerous zero captions absent from the audited PDF; its `NoncurrentInvestments = 0` sits alongside a PDF non-current investment line marked `*` (below rounding norms), not an unambiguously reported numerical zero. These observations raise a genuine ambiguity about the meaning and rounding of the machine zero; they do not prove automatic defaulting, nor do they prove that the issuer did not report zero. The attempt to collect raw context and source semantics via run 42451824428 failed before any job executed (zero jobs); no raw result from that attempt exists. The source bytes themselves were not retained, only their hashes and runner reports. Therefore, silently upgrading this specific value to a governed numeric zero, or asserting a definitive issuer nil, would be premature. The uncertainty is an unresolved evidence/adjudication blocker, not proof of absence.

The PDF page 9 extraction places the `*` and the next row's 31.45 / 214.93 on adjacent lines; those numbers are **trade receivables**, not investment values. The 48.73 investment property and 1,422.69 investments are on **standalone** PDF page 20, not the consolidated page. They must not be used to claim the consolidated XBRL contradicts those amounts. The 24 zero elements reported by the template probe are from its own broad "balance-sheet-ish" filter, which also includes cash-flow elements; they are not 24 proven balance-sheet defaults. The iXBRL parser's zero `ix facts` in the runner log is a parser limitation, not evidence that the displayed row is untagged.

## Reconsideration and safeguards

Reconsider upon obtaining the original March instance and resolving `CurrentInvestments` context, entity, instant, dimensions, currency, decimals, and whether its zero is an issuer-reported value rather than an unpopulated/rounded template field, including authoritative exchange or issuer clarification where needed. A separately reported consolidated amount for the relevant instant also permits reconsideration; a quoted explicit nil requires the separate register-owner cash-aggregation decision to close the bridge. A future D-2 distinction for absent captions would itself require authorization. Later-period values cannot be mixed with March debt and cash.

No bank balances, other financial assets, non-current investments, standalone figures, management net-debt KPI, arithmetic residual, missing caption, dash or inaccessible document was accepted as current investments or governed net debt. Existing gross debt ₹5,377.76 crore and cash ₹411.26 crore, and missing cash-and-investments and net debt, are the historical committed payload results, not a new ingestion. Strict debt-component and cash-side instant rules, D-2, IX, valuation, preference-capital and minority-interest policy were not changed. `L-038` and its evidence are untouched.
