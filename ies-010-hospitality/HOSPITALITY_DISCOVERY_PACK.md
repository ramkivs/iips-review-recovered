## 2. Sector economics

How Hospitality businesses create and earn value:
- **Primary revenue model:** room revenue (RevPAR = Occupancy × ADR), food & beverage, banquets/events, ancillary (spa, parking, retail), management/franchise fees for asset-light operators.
- **Cost structure:** labor (high), property (owned vs leased vs managed/franchised), utilities, marketing/distribution (OTA commissions), maintenance.
- **Value chain:** brand/management company → hotel/property owner → distribution (OTAs, direct, GDS) → guest. Franchise/asset-light vs owned/asset-heavy models.

## 3. Market structure

- **Segments:** Luxury, Upscale, Midscale, Economy/Budget; also Serviced Apartments, Resorts, Heritage/Boutique, Motels.
- **Business models:** Owned (REIT/asset), Managed, Franchised, Asset-light (brand licensing).
- **Drivers:** travel demand (leisure/business), tourism, RevPAR growth, ADR/Occupancy cycle, supply (new-build), brand mix.
- **Cyclical & seasonal:** highly cyclical with macro/travel cycles; strong seasonality (peak/off-peak).

## 4. Value drivers

- **Occupancy %** — utilization of room inventory.
- **ADR (Average Daily Rate)** — pricing power / rate achieved.
- **RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)** — the core operating metric (Occupancy × ADR).
- **GOP margin (Gross Operating Profit)** — operating efficiency.
- **F&B / ancillary revenue** — diversification beyond rooms.
- **Brand strength / asset-light mix** — franchise/management fee income stability.
- **What destroys value:** over-leverage on owned assets, occupancy collapse, ADR discounting, cost inflation (labor/utilities), dependence on a single segment.

## 5. Regulatory environment

- Local/regional hospitality licensing, health & safety, food safety, labor regulations.
- Zoning / land-use for new builds.
- Environmental/sustainability reporting obligations.
- Franchise/brand agreements (contract law, not financial regulation).

## 6. Lifecycle

- Industry lifecycle stage: **mature** overall, with cyclicality.
- Cyclicality / seasonality: high (macro travel cycle + seasonal peak/off-peak).
- Structural trends: asset-light migration, brand consolidation, direct-booking vs OTA mix, technology (PMS/CRM), sustainability.

## 8. Proposed methodology dimensions (candidate)

- **Franchise / asset-light quality** (earnings quality) — recurring fee income vs volatile owned operations.
- **Growth** — RevPAR growth, pipeline, expansion.
- **Risk** — leverage, cyclicality, occupancy concentration.
- **Valuation** — EV/EBITDA, P/RevPAR multiples.
- **Capital efficiency** — return on invested capital.
- **Moat / brand** — brand strength, loyalty, distribution.

## 9. Revenue Mix & Demand Quality

Revenue durability matters as much as occupancy level:
- **Business vs Leisure mix** — business demand (weekday, corporate) vs leisure (weekend, seasonal).
- **Domestic vs International demand** — dependence on cross-border/inbound travel.
- **Corporate contracts** — contracted corporate/group rates → revenue stability.
- **OTA dependence** — share of bookings via online travel agencies (higher cost, less direct relationship).
- **Direct booking ratio** — share via direct/website/brand channels (lower cost, loyalty data).
- **Group vs Transient guests** — group (banquets/events) vs transient (individual).
- **Recurring customer loyalty** — repeat guests, loyalty-program share.

These are **leading indicators of revenue durability** (demand quality), complementing pure occupancy.

## 10. Operating Leverage

Hospitality is unusually sensitive to occupancy because fixed costs are high:
- **Fixed vs variable cost base** — high fixed (property, staff, utilities) vs variable (housekeeping per occupied room).
- **Occupancy break-even** — the occupancy level at which operating costs are covered.
- **Incremental GOP** — additional gross operating profit from each occupied room above break-even.
- **Margin expansion during recovery** — GOP margin rises steeply as occupancy recovers.
- **Downside operating leverage** — margins compress sharply when occupancy falls.

This supports deterministic profitability modeling in the methodology.

## 11. Asset Strategy

Differentiate by asset ownership model — one of the biggest structural differences inside Hospitality, influencing **calibration rather than scoring logic**:
- **Owned assets** — full revenue + risk; capital-intensive.
- **Leased assets** — fixed lease cost, less capex.
- **Managed hotels** — management fee income, low capital.
- **Franchised hotels** — royalty/fee income, lowest risk, brand leverage.
- **Mixed portfolios** — blend across owned/leased/managed/franchised.

Asset-light operators (franchise/managed) have higher earnings quality + lower cyclicality.

## 12. Brand & Distribution Moat

Hospitality competitive advantages beyond property ownership:
- **Loyalty ecosystems** — repeat-guest loyalty programs.
- **Distribution network** — scale of the property/brand network.
- **Direct booking capability** — owns the guest relationship, lower OTA cost.
- **Premium brand positioning** — pricing power / ADR premium.
- **Geographic density** — cluster/density advantages in key markets.
- **Conference/event ecosystem** — group/banquet/meeting infrastructure.
- **Asset-light brand strength** — royalty stream durability.

This naturally feeds the Universal Investment Ontology's **Moat** dimension.
