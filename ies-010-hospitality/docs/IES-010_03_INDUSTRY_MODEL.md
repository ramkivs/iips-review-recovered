# Purpose

Describes the hospitality industry structure that the methodology models.

# 1. Industry segments

| Segment | Description |
|---|---|
| Luxury | premium/full-service, high ADR, high service intensity |
| Upscale | upper-mid, business+leisure |
| Midscale | balanced, cost-sensitive |
| Economy/Budget | low ADR, high occupancy orientation |
| Serviced Apartments | extended-stay |
| Resorts | destination/leisure |
| Heritage/Boutique | differentiated, experience-led |

# 2. Business models

| Model | Capital intensity | Earnings quality | Cyclicality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owned | High | Volatile (full P&L) | High |
| Leased | Medium | Fixed-lease dependent | Medium-High |
| Managed | Low | Fee income | Medium |
| Franchised | Very Low | Royalty income | Low |
| Mixed/Asset-light | Low | Recurring fees | Low |

# 3. Revenue architecture

- **Room revenue:** Occupancy × ADR (core).
- **F&B / ancillary:** banquets, events, spa, parking, retail.
- **Fee income:** management + franchise + royalty (asset-light).

# 4. Demand drivers

- Business travel, leisure travel, corporate contracts, groups/events, inbound/international.

# 5. Cyclicality & seasonality

- Highly cyclical (macro travel cycle); strong seasonality (peak/off-peak). Modeled via calibration/risk, normalized in annualized metrics.
