# Purpose

Models how hospitality businesses create, earn, and compound value.

# 1. Value creation

- **RevPAR growth** (Occupancy × ADR) that compounds.
- **Asset-light fee income** (managed/franchise) with high earnings quality.
- **Demand quality** (direct/corporate/loyalty) → revenue durability.
- **Brand + distribution moat** → pricing power + repeat demand.
- **Operating leverage** → margin expansion on recovery.
- **Efficient capital** (ROIC) on owned assets; capital recycling toward asset-light.

# 2. Value destruction

- Occupancy collapse / demand shock.
- Over-leverage on owned assets.
- ADR dilution / rate discounting.
- High OTA dependence (commission drag).
- Revenue concentration (single segment/geography).
- Unrecovered cost inflation (labor/utilities).

# 3. Revenue quality hierarchy

```text
Loyalty / Corporate Contract > Direct Booking > Travel Agency > OTA > One-off Promotional Demand
```

# 4. Capacity expansion

Organic RevPAR growth > managed additions > franchise expansion > new-build > acquisition-led.

# 5. Capital recycling

Sale-and-manage, sale-and-franchise, ROIC improvement via portfolio optimization (asset-light transition).
