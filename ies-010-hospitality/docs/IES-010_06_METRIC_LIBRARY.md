# Purpose

Defines the canonical hospitality metrics. Each metric includes definition, formula, interpretation, preferred direction, and indicative calibration bands.

# Universal metrics (all business models)

| Metric | ID | Formula | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| EBITDA Margin | HM-001 | EBITDA ÷ Revenue | Higher better |
| Revenue Growth | HM-002 | YoY revenue growth | Higher better |
| Debt/EBITDA (leverage) | HM-003 | Net Debt ÷ EBITDA | Lower better |
| Valuation | HM-004 | EV/EBITDA multiple | Contextual |

# Hospitality-specific metrics

| Metric | ID | Formula | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Occupancy | HM-005 | Rooms sold ÷ Rooms available | Higher better |
| ADR | HM-006 | Room revenue ÷ Rooms sold | Higher better |
| RevPAR | HM-007 | Room revenue ÷ Rooms available (= Occ × ADR) | Higher better |
| GOP Margin | HM-008 | GOP ÷ Total revenue | Higher better |
| RevPAR Growth | HM-009 | YoY RevPAR change | Higher better |
| Asset-light / Fee Mix | HM-010 | Fee income (mgmt+franchise) ÷ Revenue | Higher (quality) |
| Direct-book / Demand Quality | HM-011 | Direct+corporate+loyalty revenue ÷ Revenue | Higher better |
| Return on Invested Capital | HM-012 | NOPAT ÷ Invested Capital | Higher better |
