# 2. Invariant principles (recap)

1. Rate-base quality > gross size.
2. Regulatory relationship is foundational.
3. Regulated-return economics drive value.
4. Balance-sheet resilience > growth (FFO/Debt).
5. Transition is real, but must be recoverable.
6. Deterministic, replayable, explainable — platform-neutral.

# 3. Calibration vs scoring

Regulated vs merchant, segment, regulatory quality, reliability are **calibration** concerns (segment thresholds/weights), not scoring-logic changes.
