# Purpose

Defines the canonical utilities metrics. Each metric includes definition, formula, interpretation, preferred direction, and indicative calibration bands.

# Universal metrics (all segments)

| Metric | ID | Formula | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| EBITDA Margin | UM-001 | EBITDA ÷ Revenue | Higher better |
| Revenue Growth | UM-002 | YoY revenue growth | Higher better |
| Debt/EBITDA (leverage) | UM-003 | Net Debt ÷ EBITDA | Lower better |
| Valuation | UM-004 | P/E or EV/EBITDA | Contextual |
| Return on Equity | UM-005 | Net Income ÷ Equity | Higher better |

# Utilities-specific metrics

| Metric | ID | Formula | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rate Base Growth | UM-006 | YoY rate base change | Higher better |
| Allowed ROE | UM-007 | regulated return on equity | Contextual |
| FFO/Debt | UM-008 | FFO ÷ total debt | Higher better |
| O&M Efficiency | UM-009 | O&M ÷ revenue (or per customer) | Lower better |
| Demand Growth | UM-010 | YoY volume growth | Higher better |
| Reliability (SAIDI) | UM-011 | outage duration index | Lower better |
| Transition/Capex Intensity | UM-012 | grid/renewables capex share | Higher (recoverable) |

# Calibration bands (examples)

## UM-006 Rate Base Growth (higher better)
| Range | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| > 8% | Strong |
| 5–8% | Good |
| 2–5% | Adequate |
| < 2% | Weak |

## UM-008 FFO/Debt (higher better)
| Range | Interpretation |
|---|---|

| < 10% | Weak |
