# IES-013 — Consumer Portfolio Architecture

**Standard:** IES-013 — Consumer Sector Engine
**Phase:** 2 — Engineering Standard (conceptual)
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Status:** METHODOLOGY FRAMEWORK

> **Purpose.** Consumer companies compete through multiple, interacting brands and product portfolios. These concepts influence calibration, not scoring logic, and map into the Engineering Standard.

---

## 1. Consumer portfolio concepts

| Concept | Description |
|---|---|
| **Flagship brands** | the lead brand(s) driving revenue/profit |
| **Premium vs value portfolio** | mix of premium (margin) vs value (volume) brands |
| **Category diversification** | exposure across categories (food, personal care, etc.) |
| **Geographic diversification** | spread across regions/markets |
| **Channel diversification** | retail, e-commerce/DTC, wholesale, foodservice |
| **Private-label exposure** | share of revenue at risk to retailers' private label |
| **Innovation cadence** | rate of new-product launches/renewal |
| **Portfolio rationalization** | pruning/streamlining of weak brands |

## 2. Calibration influence

- **Premium vs value mix** affects margin/quality calibration.
- **Category/geographic/channel diversification** reduces risk (risk calibration).
- **Private-label exposure** is a risk/quality consideration.
- **Innovation cadence** supports growth.
