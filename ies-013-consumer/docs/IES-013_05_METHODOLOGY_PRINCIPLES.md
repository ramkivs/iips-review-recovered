# 2. Invariant principles (recap)

1. Brand + pricing power > gross size.
2. Demand durability is foundational (staple vs discretionary).
3. Distribution is access to demand.
4. Margin resilience > peak margin.
5. Capital discipline > growth.
6. Deterministic, replayable, explainable — platform-neutral.

# 3. Calibration vs scoring

Segment (staples/discretionary/luxury), business model, portfolio architecture are **calibration** concerns, not scoring-logic changes.
