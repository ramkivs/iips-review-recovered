# Purpose

Defines the canonical consumer metrics. Each metric includes definition, formula, interpretation, preferred direction, and indicative calibration bands.

# Universal metrics (all segments)

| Metric | ID | Formula | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| EBITDA Margin | CM-001 | EBITDA ÷ Revenue | Higher better |
| Revenue Growth | CM-002 | YoY revenue growth | Higher better |
| Debt/EBITDA (leverage) | CM-003 | Net Debt ÷ EBITDA | Lower better |
| Valuation | CM-004 | P/E or EV/EBITDA | Contextual |
| Return on Invested Capital | CM-005 | NOPAT ÷ Invested Capital | Higher better |

# Consumer-specific metrics

| Metric | ID | Formula | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pricing Power / Volume Mix | CM-006 | price contribution vs volume | Higher (price) |
| Brand / Loyalty | CM-007 | repeat purchase, market share | Higher better |
| Margin Resilience | CM-008 | margin stability / input pass-through | Higher better |
| Distribution / Channel Mix | CM-009 | DTC/direct share | Higher (quality) |
| FCF Yield | CM-010 | FCF ÷ market cap | Higher better |
| Innovation Intensity | CM-011 | new-product revenue share | Higher (growth) |
| Private-label Exposure | CM-012 | private-label revenue share | Lower (risk) |

# Calibration bands (examples)

## CM-001 EBITDA Margin (higher better)
| Range | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| > 25% | Strong |
| 15–25% | Good |
| 8–15% | Adequate |
| < 8% | Weak |

## CM-002 Revenue Growth (higher better)
| Range | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| > 10% | Strong |
| 5–10% | Good |
| 2–5% | Adequate |
| < 2% | Weak |
