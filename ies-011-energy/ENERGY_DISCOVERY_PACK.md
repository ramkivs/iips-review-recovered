## 2. Sector economics

How Energy businesses create and earn value:
- **Primary revenue model:** commodity price × volume (oil, gas, power), with integrated (upstream+downstream), E&P, midstream (transport/storage), and utilities/power segments.
- **Cost structure:** capital-intensive (upstream exploration, refining, power generation), operating leverage to commodity prices.
- **Value chain:** Upstream (E&P) → Midstream (transport/processing/storage) → Downstream (refining/marketing) → Power generation → Retail/utilities.

## 3. Market structure

- **Segments:** Integrated Oil & Gas, E&P (exploration & production), Oilfield Services, Midstream (pipelines/LNG/storage), Refining & Marketing, Independent Power Producers (IPP), Utilities (regulated), Renewables (solar/wind).
- **Commodity exposure:** oil, natural gas, electricity, LNG, coal, carbon.
- **Drivers:** commodity prices (supply/demand), OPEC+/geopolitics, energy transition, demand growth, infrastructure, regulation.

## 4. Value drivers

- **Commodity price realization** (oil/gas/power) — the dominant top-line driver.
- **Production / volumes** — reserve life, output growth.
- **Reserves & resource base** — reserve replacement, resource life.
- **Cost per barrel / lifting cost** — cash cost efficiency.
- **Margin** (downstream/refining, midstream tariffs).
- **Energy transition positioning** — renewables/gas vs legacy.
- **What destroys value:** cost overruns, reserve write-downs, price collapse, over-leverage, project delays, stranded assets.

## 5. Regulatory environment

- Hydrocarbon licensing/leases, environmental (emissions/carbon), safety (upstream/refining), utilities rate regulation, renewable mandates/subsidies.
- OPEC+/national policy, commodity export controls, carbon pricing.

## 6. Lifecycle

- Industry lifecycle stage: **mature** (oil/gas) with **growth** (renewables).
- Cyclicality: **high** (commodity price cycles).
- Structural trends: energy transition, decarbonization, gas as transition fuel, renewables scaling, electrification.

## 10. Capital Allocation & Resource Replacement

Energy has a distinctive long-duration capital cycle. Key concepts (likely foundational methodology principles, not implementation details):

- **Reserve replacement** — replacing produced reserves via discovery/acquisition (reserve-replacement ratio).
- **Depletion** — the natural decline of producing reserves/assets over time.
- **Exploration success** — success rate and cost of adding new reserves.
- **Sustaining vs growth capex** — maintenance capex to keep output vs expansionary capex.
- **Project payback** — long payback horizons on major projects (upstream, LNG, power).
- **Commodity-cycle capital discipline** — investing counter-cyclically vs pro-cyclically; capital allocation across the price cycle.

This long-duration, capital-replacement dynamic is a core Energy methodology principle.
