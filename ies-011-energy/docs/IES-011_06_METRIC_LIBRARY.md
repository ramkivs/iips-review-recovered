# Purpose

Defines the canonical energy metrics. Each metric includes definition, formula, interpretation, preferred direction, and indicative calibration bands.

# Universal metrics (all segments)

| Metric | ID | Formula | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| EBITDA Margin | EM-001 | EBITDA ÷ Revenue | Higher better |
| Revenue Growth | EM-002 | YoY revenue growth | Higher better |
| Debt/EBITDA (leverage) | EM-003 | Net Debt ÷ EBITDA | Lower better |
| Valuation | EM-004 | EV/EBITDA multiple | Contextual |
| Return on Capital | EM-005 | ROCE / ROIC | Higher better |

# Energy-specific metrics

| Metric | ID | Formula | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Production Growth | EM-006 | YoY output change | Higher better |
| Lifting Cost / Cash Cost | EM-007 | cost per unit (per boe/MWh) | Lower better |
| Reserve Replacement | EM-008 | reserves added ÷ produced | Higher better |
| Reserve Life | EM-009 | reserves ÷ annual production | Higher (contextual) |
| Realized Price | EM-010 | realized commodity price | Contextual |
| Transition / Renewables Mix | EM-011 | clean share of capacity/revenue | Higher (quality) |
| FCF Yield | EM-012 | FCF ÷ market cap | Higher better |
