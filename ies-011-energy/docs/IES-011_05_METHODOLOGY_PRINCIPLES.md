# 2. Invariant principles (recap)

1. Cost position > size.
2. Reserve replacement sustains value.
3. Capital discipline > growth.
4. Commodity cycle is structural (risk via calibration, normalized valuation).
5. Energy transition is a real quality/risk signal.
6. Deterministic, replayable, explainable — platform-neutral.

# 3. Calibration vs scoring

Value chain position, commodity exposure, cyclicality are **calibration** concerns (segment thresholds/weights), not scoring-logic changes.
